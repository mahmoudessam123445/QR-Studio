Frequently Asked Questions

Is QR Studio really free?

Yes, completely free. No hidden fees, no premium tiers, no subscriptions. Everything is available to everyone.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no signup, no login, and no authentication required. Just open the app and start generating.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All QR codes are generated entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No data is ever sent to any server. Your information stays on your device.

Can I use this offline?

Yes! After the first load, the entire application works without an internet connection. Perfect for travel or secure environments.

What image formats can I download?

You can download your QR codes as high-resolution PNG images or as scalable SVG vectors. Both support up to 2048px resolution.

Can I add my logo to the QR code?

Yes! Upload any square image as a center logo. We recommend keeping the logo small (under 100x100px) to maintain scannability.

Where is my QR code history stored?

Your history is saved in your browser's LocalStorage. It never leaves your device. You can clear it anytime from the History page.

What QR code types are supported?

We support 9 types: URL, Plain Text, WiFi, Phone, WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Google Maps Location, and vCard Contact.
